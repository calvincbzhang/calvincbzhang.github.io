I am currently a research intern at [CHAI](https://humancompatible.ai/), where I work with [Micah Carroll](https://micahcarroll.github.io/) on reinforcement learning and robustness of large language models.

Before joining CHAI, I was a visiting scholar at [MIT](https://mit.edu/), where I focused on online learning and reward design for reinforcement learning. During that time, I was fortunate to collaborate with [Zhang-Wei Hong](https://williamd4112.github.io/), [Aldo Pacchiano](https://www.aldopacchiano.ai/), and [Pulkit Agrawal](https://people.csail.mit.edu/pulkitag/).

I hold an MSc in Data Science from [ETH Zurich](https://ethz.ch/en.html), where I worked with [Giorgia Ramponi](https://gioramponi.github.io/) on preference-based reinforcement learning.

Earlier, I completed my BSc (Hons) in Computer Science and Mathematics at the [University of Manchester](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/), where I researched adversarial attacks in deep reinforcement learning under the supervision of [Tingting Mu](https://personalpages.manchester.ac.uk/staff/tingting.mu/Site/About_Me.html).

I am interested in **safe generalization in sequential decision making** and **AI alignment**, with applications to **autonomous agents** and **robotics**. I aim to develop algorithms that enable agents to make reliable and safe decisions over long time horizons, even in complex and unpredictable environments.
