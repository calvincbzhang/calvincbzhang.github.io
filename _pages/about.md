I am the ML Research Ops Lead at [Scale AI](https://scale.com/), where I focus on designing and running evaluations, developing benchmarks, and managing leaderboards.

Previously, I was a research intern at [CHAI](https://humancompatible.ai/), where I worked with [Micah Carroll](https://micahcarroll.github.io/) on multi-turn red teaming strategies for large language models. I also spent time as a visiting scholar at [MIT](https://mit.edu/), where I focused on online learning and reward design for reinforcement learning, working with [Zhang-Wei Hong](https://williamd4112.github.io/), [Aldo Pacchiano](https://www.aldopacchiano.ai/), and [Pulkit Agrawal](https://people.csail.mit.edu/pulkitag/).

I received my MSc in Data Science from [ETH Zurich](https://ethz.ch/en.html), where I conducted reserach in preference-based reinforcement learning with [Giorgia Ramponi](https://gioramponi.github.io/).

Earlier, I completed my BSc (Hons) in Computer Science and Mathematics from the [University of Manchester](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/), with a focus on adversarial attacks in deep reinforcement learning under the supervision of [Tingting Mu](https://personalpages.manchester.ac.uk/staff/tingting.mu/Site/About_Me.html).

My research interests include **evaluation methodologies**, **agents**, **sequential decision making**, and **AI safety and alignment**.
