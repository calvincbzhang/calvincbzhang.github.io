I am currently a research intern at [CHAI](https://humancompatible.ai/), where I work with [Micah Carroll](https://micahcarroll.github.io/) on reinforcement learning and the robustness of large language models.

Before joining CHAI, I was a visiting scholar at [MIT](https://mit.edu/), where I focused on online learning and reward design for reinforcement learning. During that time, I collaborated with [Zhang-Wei Hong](https://williamd4112.github.io/), [Aldo Pacchiano](https://www.aldopacchiano.ai/), and [Pulkit Agrawal](https://people.csail.mit.edu/pulkitag/).

I hold an MSc in Data Science from [ETH Zurich](https://ethz.ch/en.html), where I worked with [Giorgia Ramponi](https://gioramponi.github.io/) on preference-based reinforcement learning.

Earlier, I completed my BSc (Hons) in Computer Science and Mathematics at the [University of Manchester](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/), where I researched adversarial attacks in deep reinforcement learning under the supervision of [Tingting Mu](https://personalpages.manchester.ac.uk/staff/tingting.mu/Site/About_Me.html).

My research interests lie in **sequential decision making**, **robustness**, and **AI alignment**, with a focus on their applications to **autonomous agents** and **robotics**. I am particularly interested in how reinforcement learning algorithms can be made more resilient to adversarial conditions and environmental uncertainties, and how AI systems can be designed to align with human values and safety requirements. My goal is to develop algorithms that enable agents to make reliable decisions over long time horizons, even in complex and unpredictable environments.
