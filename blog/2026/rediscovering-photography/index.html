Rediscovering Photography

Finding my way back to photography and learning to look again.

In I Am Assigning Myself Homework, I said I would write more about random topics that tickle my curiosity. I guess I’ve been a bad student this summer. But I have rediscovered an old passion of mine: photography.

When I was at university, I picked up photography as a hobby and started shooting portraits and events. My first ever camera was a Canon EOS Rebel T7i, or an 800D, as it was called in the UK, where I bought it. It was a great camera to start with. I spent countless hours culling and editing my photos, watching tutorials from photographers I admired, and experimenting with composition and my camera’s settings. As I gained confidence, I upgraded the kit lens to a 17–55mm f/2.8 zoom and the trusty nifty fifty, 50mm f/1.8.

Then life got busy. Moving countries, grad school, starting a new job in a new country. Somewhere along the way, I almost forgot I had a camera

This summer, I went to New York and found myself looking left and right, up and down, and taking photos again, mostly with my phone. It reminded me of why I liked photography in the first place. It makes you more observant. You can find beauty in the mundane. And it’s a great creative outlet for someone like me: there’s a lot to learn on the technical side, but also an unlimited amount of creative freedom.

While I was there, I had to stop by B&H and check out the store. I ended up trading in my DSLR and finally upgrading to a mirrorless camera. I’ve taken it out a few times since, and I’m rediscovering how much I enjoy photography, but I also feel like my taste has changed. I used to chase technically “correct” or conventionally “beautiful” photos: a perfect bokeh or a super sharp subject. Now I’m gravitating more towards street photography, which has led to a new learning experience.

It feels very much in line with il fanciullino: a sense of wonder when experiencing the world and the pleasure of learning to look again. I thought I would find my way back to that through writing, but instead, this summer it happened through a forgotten hobby of mine

I am still retraining my photographer’s eye and experimenting with different ways to edit my photos, but I thought I would share a few recent shots.

Until next time.
